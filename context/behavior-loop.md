The robot on its own, once put down (the maintainer's design): find a person; if there is one, walk up in one curve to about 1 m facing them, "talk", notice when the talk is over, turn 180° away; if not, walk to a point in front of it; then again. People are noticed all along, so a walk to a point is cut short for someone worth walking up to. One node, `harp/behavior.py`, started by `entrypoint.sh` for `G1_TASK_COMMAND` `{"behavior": {...}}` in place of `course_goal.py`; it drives only through Nav2, so `approach_stop.py` still stands between Nav2 and our node ([[approach-stop]]); a task with `"avoidance": "predictive"` is accepted but the loop is untested with that filter ([[predictive-avoidance]]). It reuses `course_goal.py`'s Nav2 client (`go_to` with an `interrupt`, which cancels the goal). Its states, settings and events are in its docstring.

**Placeholders** (the maintainer asked for the full loop before the detector and speech exist, 2026-10-30)
- **Person detection:** `harp/sim_people.py`, sim only, the shape of a real detector: the lidar finds person *candidates* by their shape, the camera confirms a *person* by their legs. Candidates are every mover (all people) and every fixed upright cylinder or box 0.2–0.7 m across and 1.0–2.1 m tall on the floor (a decoy, such as a pillar, which the camera never confirms). Each is found only where the robot's real sensors could (coverage from [[g1-head-camera]] and [[seeing-ahead-research]]): by the lidar where its head leaves it a view (`harp/lidar_mount.py`'s visor setting, [[g1-face-visor]]; design for the visor off: ahead and past about 60° to the sides, not in the wedges between) out to 11 m, and by the pitched-down camera only by the legs, in its colour view and within 2 m. Each needs a clear ray (lidar at 0.6/1.0/1.4 m above the floor, camera to the legs); ±5 cm; JSON on `/harp/people` in `base_link`, with a stable id (a tracker's) and `seen_by`, the sensors that see it now. A real lidar-and-camera detector should publish the same message; `behavior.py` then needs no change.
- **Talk:** through speech when its bridge answers (`speech/bridge.py`, the handshake on `/harp/talk`: [[talking]], built 2026-11-11). A `prepare` goes out once the camera confirms the person it walks up to (the final goal), `start` on arriving; it then stands until speech's `talk_done` (`source` speech), the person leaving, or 240 s (`LONGEST`), sending `stop` with the reason, and a failed walk up sends `stop` (`cancelled`). Until speech sends `talk_start`, and always in the sim (no speech there), the placeholder: standing until the person leaves (2 s out of sight or 2 m away) or 30 s pass, the stand-in for them saying goodbye (the maintainer's numbers, 2026-10-30). Its `talk_done` says `speech` (whether speech had it) and `ended`: speech's reason (`bye`, `goodbye`, `time`, `misses`, `stopped`, `failed`) or `left`/`time`, with speech's `error` if it failed.

**Design reasons**
- Only people standing still are walked up to (under 0.3 m/s over 1 s): someone walking past is busy, and `approach_stop.py` stands the robot while they come at it. A person who moves 0.5 m from where it aims gets a new goal, up to 3 times.
- Check, then confirm, in one walk (the maintainer's design): a lidar candidate gets a goal 1.5 m out (`CHECK`), facing it, where the camera sees legs (the goal checker stops it about 1.75 m out). Once the camera confirms them, `go_to`'s `handoff` leaves that goal driving and the final goal (0.85 m) replaces it, so Nav2 takes over without stopping: the speed dips from about 0.23 to 0.14–0.16 m/s for 1–2 s and never stops (`decoys`, `people_ahead`). Not confirmed within 2 s (`CONFIRM`) of reaching the check goal: `not_a_person`, left alone like anyone for 120 s. Someone in the lidar's blind wedges is lost on the way: it keeps to where it last saw them (a standing person), and the camera has them again for the confirmation, the talk and noticing they've left.
- Wander points are in front of the robot (`MIN_LEG`–`MAX_LEG`, within `FRONT` of its heading), so it walks on ahead, and after a turn away, on away from the person. They stay within `radius` of the start in `odom`, so it doesn't walk off: at the edge, facing out, the cone widens until a point fits; from outside (after walking up to someone beyond it) it walks back into the area, to the start only when no point within reach is inside it. There is no map, so a point can land in an obstacle; Nav2 then fails or times out (60 s) and the next point is taken. A seed (`people.yaml`) makes the points repeat between runs.
- The approach goal is 0.85 m from the person's centre: the goal checker (0.25 m, 20°) stops the robot as it comes within reach, so it ends about 1.1 m away (a 1 m goal ended 1.16–1.32 m). The goal stays 0.6 m from their surface, outside Nav2's lethal 0.4 m (`robot_radius`), so NavFn plans to it.
- The turn away is a Nav2 goal where the robot stands, heading flipped: the Graceful Controller only turns within `goal_dist_tolerance`, so nothing but Nav2 drives.
- Nav2 can report `SUCCEEDED` at once for a goal sent right after another finished (a wander goal 1.15 m away, 0.05 s, once in `runs/2026-11-02_20-03-13Z_sim_people_wander-ahead`), the controller following the old path (`navigate.xml` now plans first, [[nav2-sim]]). For an approach goal that would start the talk from where it stood, so `Behavior.go` still sends a goal again when Nav2 calls it reached with the robot over 0.4 m or 30° from it (a `resend` event).
- Not walked up to again for 120 s (by id). A real tracker's ids may not last that long.

**Robot:** not yet: `entrypoint.sh` starts the loop only in a world's image in the sim, like Nav2 and `approach_stop.py`, which haven't run on the robot ([[nav2-sim]], [[safety]]); our image can't read the depth camera there either ([[sim-vs-real]]). On the robot the loop would start after D-pad Left + B (HIM's handover).

**Scoring:** `course.py people` (the task is in `people.yaml`): summary `loop` with each talk's true distance to that person and facing error (medians over 3 s from 0.5 s into the talk, before anyone leaves) and how it ended, wander goals, people noticed, farthest from the start; `min_talks`, `talk_distance_m`, `max_talk_facing_deg`, `first_talk` (who), `max_first_talk_s` (from the loop's start) and `not_people` (candidates it must pass over as `not_a_person` and never talk to) criteria; `decoys` puts a pillar nearer than a person. `people_ahead` tests the visor-off view (its xml says how): it fails `first_talk` with the visor on, by design. Events carry no sim time, so each is timed from the last `odom` line before it plus the wall-clock time since. Talk distances below are the scored true ones.
