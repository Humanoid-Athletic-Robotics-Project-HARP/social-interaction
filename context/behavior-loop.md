The robot on its own: wander, notice a person, walk up in one curve to about 1 m facing them, "talk", turn 180° away, then the loop again (the maintainer's design, 2026-10-30). One node, `harp/behavior.py`, started by `entrypoint.sh` for `G1_TASK_COMMAND` `{"behavior": {...}}` in place of `course_goal.py`; it drives only through Nav2, so `approach_stop.py` still stands between Nav2 and our node ([[nav2-sim]]). It reuses `course_goal.py`'s Nav2 client (`go_to` with an `interrupt`, which cancels the goal). Its states, settings and events are in its docstring.

**Placeholders** (the maintainer asked for the full loop before the detector and speech exist, 2026-10-30)
- **Person detection:** `harp/sim_people.py`, sim only: every mover is a person, found only where the robot's real sensors could find them (the maintainer's call, 2026-11-02; coverage from [[g1-head-camera]] and [[seeing-ahead-research]]): by the lidar where its head leaves it a view (`harp/lidar_mount.py`'s visor setting, [[g1-face-visor]]: visor off, ahead and past about 60° to the sides, not in the wedges between; on, only past about 60°) out to 11 m, and by the pitched-down camera only by the legs, in its colour view and within 2 m, where leg detection works. With the visor on, nobody farther than about 2 m ahead is seen. Each needs a clear ray (lidar to their body at 0.6/1.0/1.4 m, camera to the legs); ±5 cm; JSON on `/harp/people` in `base_link`, with a stable id (a tracker's) and `seen_by`. A real lidar-and-camera detector should publish the same message; `behavior.py` then needs no change.
- **Talk:** a `talk` event with the line it would say, then standing until the person leaves (2 s out of sight or 2 m away) or 30 s pass, the stand-in for them saying goodbye (the maintainer's numbers, 2026-10-30). Speech goes there ([[g1-builtin-apis]] for Unitree's TTS); a goodbye heard would end it too.

**Design reasons**
- Only people standing still are walked up to (under 0.3 m/s over 1 s): someone walking past is busy, and `approach_stop.py` stands the robot while they come at it. A person who moves 0.5 m from where it aims gets a new goal, up to 3 times.
- With the visor on, people are mostly noticed off to the side, and the approach goal turns the robot toward them, where neither sensor sees them until about 2 m: it keeps to where it last saw them (a standing person), and the camera has them again for the talk and for noticing they've left. With it off the lidar sees them ahead too, except in the wedges either side.
- Wander points are in `odom` within a radius of the start: there is no map, so a point can land in an obstacle; Nav2 then fails or times out (60 s) and the next point is taken. A seed (`people.yaml`) makes the points repeat between runs.
- The approach goal is 0.85 m from the person's centre: the goal checker (0.25 m, 20°) stops the robot as it comes within reach, so it ends about 1.1 m away (a 1 m goal ended 1.16–1.32 m). The goal stays 0.6 m from their surface, outside Nav2's lethal 0.4 m (`robot_radius`), so NavFn plans to it.
- The turn away is a Nav2 goal where the robot stands, heading flipped: the Graceful Controller only turns within `goal_dist_tolerance`, so nothing but Nav2 drives. The next wander point is any point; only the turn is required.
- Not walked up to again for 120 s (by id). A real tracker's ids may not last that long.

**Robot:** not yet: `entrypoint.sh` starts the loop only in a world's image in the sim, like Nav2 and `approach_stop.py`, which haven't run on the robot ([[nav2-sim]], [[safety]]); our image can't read the depth camera there either ([[sim-vs-real]]). On the robot the loop would start after D-pad Left + B (HIM's handover).

**Scoring:** `course.py people` (the task is in `people.yaml`): summary `loop` with each talk's true distance to that person and facing error (medians over its first 3 s, before anyone leaves) and how it ended, wander goals, people noticed, farthest from the start; `min_talks`, `talk_distance_m`, `max_talk_facing_deg`, `first_talk` (who) and `max_first_talk_s` (from the loop's start) criteria. `people_ahead` tests the visor-off view (its xml says how): it fails `first_talk` with the visor on, by design. Events carry no sim time, so each is timed by the last `odom`/`timing` line before it (within 1 s).

**Results, visor off** (2026-11-02, `runs/2026-11-02_06-25-39Z_sim_people_visor-off`, passed): two wander legs, then person_a (noticed at 2.9 m) and person_b (3.2 m); talks at 1.10–1.12 m, facing within 6°; person_a's ended as they walked off (18.5 s), person_b's at 30 s; then three wander legs without meeting person_c before the 150 s ended; 0 contacts, closest 0.46 m, drift 2.5 cm.

**people_ahead** (2026-11-02): visor off (`runs/2026-11-02_07-37-45Z_sim_people_ahead_visor-off-3`) passed: person_ahead noticed at 3.43 m as the loop started, talk at 1.17 m 11.5 s in; after the turn away person_wedge noticed at 2.0 m, talk at 1.13 m; 0 contacts, closest 0.47 m. Visor on (`runs/2026-11-02_07-40-19Z_sim_people_ahead_visor-on`) failed only `first_talk`, as meant: person_wedge first, noticed 1.7 s in once the first wander leg turned the robot. With HIM's live viewer a run was stopped during the second turn (`inconclusive`): run it without `--view` on the 16 GB Mac.

**Results, visor on** (2026-11-02, `runs/2026-11-02_06-02-47Z_sim_people_loop-real-sensors`, 150 s, passed, before the visor setting: lidar finding people only past 60°): one wander leg, then all three people, each first seen by the lidar off to the side at 3.0–4.0 m; talks at 1.09–1.19 m, facing within 7°; person_a's ended as they walked off (22 s), person_b's at 30 s, person_c's when the run ended; turns away about 6 s; 0 contacts, closest 0.40 m, drift 2 cm. In `people` everyone starts just over 4 m away (the lidar would otherwise find them at once), and person_a, the first met with this seed, walks off at a fixed time (movers can't react): someone first noticed near the 4 m edge can change the order between runs (an earlier layout met person_a too late). Run it without `--view` on the 16 GB Mac: with HIM's live viewer, 3 of 5 runs lost their trace at the end ([[sim-deadline-stops]]).
